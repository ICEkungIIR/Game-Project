The loop

Farm crops and shy monsters, turn what you gather into something worth selling, then carry it to the tavern.

◆

Farm the plot

Till, plant, water, harvest — four crops growing side by side: wheat, carrot, potato, tomato.

◆

Coax a monster home

Wandering creatures roam the fields. Get close, hold still, and try your luck at bringing one into the pen.

◆

Cook something worth eating

Flour becomes bread, vegetables become soup — recipes that turn a raw harvest into a finished dish.

◆

Sell by lamplight

Bring your goods to the tavern's vendor and walk away with coin for the next day's seeds.
